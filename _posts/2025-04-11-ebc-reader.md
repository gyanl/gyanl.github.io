Founded in 1942, the EBC Group is a giant in Indian legal publishing. We helped them reimagine the brand and experience for their e-Library offering, EBC Reader, for an AI-first era.

The work was a visual and strategic overhaul: introducing LLM capabilities into the product and rethinking content discovery for legal professionals. Bringing a language model into a legal research tool is a trust problem before it's an interface problem — the design has to make clear what the machine did and where the source sits.

See it live at [www.ebcreader.com](https://www.ebcreader.com/).
