sā Biennale sits at the intersection of land art and climate activism — Asia's highest land art festival, staged at altitude in the Himalayas.

We crafted the branding and design for it. Identity work for a festival held in a landscape that dramatic comes with an obvious trap: anything you design will be photographed against mountains, and the mountains will win. The system had to be confident enough to hold its own and restrained enough not to compete with the reason everyone came.
