The Anant Foundation for the Arts supports modern and contemporary South Asian art practices. We designed and built their website.

Designing for an arts foundation means getting out of the way. The work on the site is the point, and the job of everything around it is to frame without commenting — which is much harder to do well than it is to describe.

See it live at [anantfoundation.art](https://anantfoundation.art).
