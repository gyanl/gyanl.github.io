We partnered with [Hammock Mag](https://map.hammockmag.com) to bring their editorial archive to life through an interactive map that visualises the geographical journey of the authors they've published. The goal was a discovery tool that merged design, development and content strategy in a way that honoured the magazine's thoughtful tone and experimental spirit.

### How it was built

The tool is a responsive web app with a clean, immersive interface. We worked with GeoJSON datasets and a custom CMS workflow that let Hammock's editors tag stories with meaningful metadata — author location, themes, timeframes. Readers explore these narratives spatially, uncovering patterns across regions and literary movements.

### Design considerations

We emphasised minimalism and legibility: generous white space, serif typography, organic animations. Technically, we optimised for fast loads and mobile browsing while preserving the editorial richness of the original stories.
