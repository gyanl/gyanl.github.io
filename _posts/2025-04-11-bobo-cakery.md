As a boutique bakery, Bobo Cakery could bake you any cake you wanted, and there was a lot of scope for customisation. We wanted to maintain the ability to pick your dream cake — but with an interface that wasn't just text boxes.

We mapped the offerings first: standard designs, decor cakes, and fully custom work. The flow was built around that tiering, with clear product types, up-front pricing indicators, and guidance embedded in the order process rather than a blank brief to fill in. Low-ticket enquiries run through the website, while premium custom orders move to a WhatsApp consultation, so the bakery's own time doesn't become the bottleneck.

On the back end the team runs Shopify. We streamlined their product architecture, adjusted theme code to improve speed, and tidied the CMS setup so they could update offerings without technical support.

See it live at [bobocakery.com](https://bobocakery.com/).
