A brand refresh and a Framer website for Freestand, who build sampling solutions for FMCG brands across India.

I'd designed [Freestand's original logo](https://gyanl.com/freestand) back in 2021, so coming back to the same brand years later with a studio behind me was a strange and satisfying loop to close. The refresh kept what still worked and rebuilt the rest around a business that had grown considerably in the meantime.

See it live at [freestand.in](https://freestand.in/).
