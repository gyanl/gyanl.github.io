SCC Online is one of India's most widely used legal research databases, housing millions of documents spanning case law, statutory material and international legal resources. Published by Eastern Book Company, it's relied on daily by advocates, judges and academic institutions across the country.

We provided branding and product design, helping refine the digital experience of the platform. Legal research tools are an interesting design problem: the users are experts, the density is non-negotiable, and the cost of a wrong result is high. You can't simplify your way out of it — you have to make the complexity navigable.

That work extended to the journals themselves. Company Cases is a weekly in six volumes covering corporate law, banking and insurance — one of the most-referred journals of its class — and we gave it a new mark and cover system that works in print and on screen.
