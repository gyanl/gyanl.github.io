NeuroSonic is a medical device that makes stroke prevention faster, cheaper and dramatically more accessible. We designed and built the landing page.

Medical device marketing runs on a narrow rail — you have to be compelling to clinicians and investors while staying accurate about what the device does and doesn't claim. The restraint is the design.
