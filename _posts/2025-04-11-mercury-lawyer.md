Mercury Lawyer monitors Indian courts and delivers case alerts to lawyers within minutes of publication. The platform gives them a single workspace for tracking hearings, reviewing orders and managing case information across court complexes.

We contributed the brand identity and the UI/UX, shaping how legal professionals interact with the real-time tracking and notification features. The design challenge is urgency without alarm — an alert system that a lawyer checks many times a day has to convey what changed, fast, without turning the whole interface into a siren.

![The Mercury Lawyer identity and product screens, including Instant Order Summaries, Mercury Ask and the Draft Assistant](/assets/pks-mercury-lawyer-board.jpg)

###### The identity, and the product surfaces it lives on.

An earlier route didn't make it: a paper-plane mark with a lowercase serif wordmark, shown here reversed out of a blue gradient and again in black. The identity that shipped went the other way — a hard-edged geometric mark and a bold grotesque.
