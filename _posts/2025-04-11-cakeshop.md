Design explorations and identity for Zomato Cakeshop — a look at how dessert ordering could delight a broader audience than the occasion-driven buyers it usually reaches.

Cake is almost entirely an occasion purchase, which caps how often anyone opens the app. Most of the interesting thinking went into whether that ceiling is real or just a habit of how the category has always been merchandised.
