Advanced Hydrocarbon Fuels Limited developed a patented chemical process that converts plastic waste into clean-burning fuel. The science was sound and the innovation promising. The messaging was dense, technical, and inaccessible to anyone outside the lab.

![Advanced Hydrocarbon Fuels horizontal monochrome logo](https://gyanl.com/assets/pks-AHFL-Horiz-Mono.png)

They needed help translating the breakthrough into a story investors, policymakers and the public could follow — one that felt as compelling as the science behind it.

### The approach

We started by learning the process ourselves. Through deep-dive workshops with AHFL's engineers and scientists, we mapped the lifecycle of the technology, from raw plastic to refined fuel, and then distilled it into three narratives:

- **The environmental story.** Turning a global waste problem into a renewable energy solution.
- **The business opportunity.** A scalable model with global market potential.
- **The scientific edge.** A process that is efficient, patented, and already in pilot deployment.

Each thread was supported by clear infographics, iconography, and a visual identity rooted in industrial precision. The goal was never to oversimplify — it was to make the extraordinary understandable.

### What we delivered

A pitch deck for investors and government partnerships, explainer diagrams of the conversion process, a visual identity system for the technology, web content structure and tone guidelines, and technical illustrations for whitepapers and regulatory submissions.

The visual system is now used across the company's investor relations, partner onboarding and regulatory presentations.
