{%- comment -%} The stickers ringing the hero mark. The hero IS an iMessage typing indicator, so the things orbiting it are the stickers that thread would send — which is why they belong here and nowhere else on the page. Each one is placed by a direction vector rather than an angle, so the ring can be an ellipse fitted to the viewport (--ring-x by --ring-y in main.css) instead of a circle that would run off the top and bottom of a landscape window. --dx and --dy are that direction, and also the line each one travels out along as the page scrolls, so a sticker leaves the frame straight out from the centre. Roughly even around the clock, with two gaps left on purpose: nothing sits where the typing bubble hangs (just up and right of the mark's centre) and nothing sits at bottom dead centre, which is the scroll cue's. --shadow-x/--shadow-y are the sticker's own shadow offset, eyeballed off its --dx/--dy rather than computed from them: a downward drop with a nudge away from the centre, so the ring reads as lit from the middle and spreading out. The two at the top of the ring carry .hero-sticker--tip-up: their notes hang above them rather than below, so a note does not drop across the mark in the middle. --sticker-scale is doing real work here, not decoration. The art is sized by HEIGHT (.hero-sticker__art is height: 100%, width: auto), so at an equal scale a 2.6:1 sticker draws two and a half times the width of a square one and swamps the ring. Each scale below is roughly sqrt(0.8 / aspect), which evens the stickers out by area instead of by height. The two wide ones are placed left and right, where the ellipse is widest and there is room for them. The ring is NOT aria-hidden any more. Each sticker is a real button — hover on a mouse, tap on a touchscreen — and a focusable control inside an aria-hidden subtree is a tab stop nobody is told about. The button carries the note as its accessible name, so it is read once; the visible bubble is hidden from AT to avoid saying it twice, and the art keeps alt="" because the note already describes it. {%- endcomment -%}
