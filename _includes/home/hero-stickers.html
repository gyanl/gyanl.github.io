{%- comment -%} The stickers ringing the hero mark. These are the Zomato iMessage pack (see /lettuce-party), cut out of the sheet at assets/stickers.png — which is why they belong here and nowhere else on the page: the hero IS an iMessage typing indicator, so the things orbiting it are the stickers that thread would send. Each one is placed by a direction vector rather than an angle, so the ring can be an ellipse fitted to the viewport (--ring-x by --ring-y in main.css) instead of a circle that would run off the top and bottom of a landscape window. --dx and --dy are that direction; the same vector is what they travel along as the page scrolls, so each sticker leaves the frame straight out from the centre. Roughly even around the clock, with two gaps left on purpose: nothing sits where the typing bubble hangs (just up and right of the mark's centre) and nothing sits at bottom dead centre, which is the scroll cue's. aria-hidden with empty alt: the whole hero is decorative, and the page's real h1 already carries the name. {%- endcomment -%}
