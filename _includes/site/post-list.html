{%- comment -%} Shared post listing for the archive and tag pages. Caller sets `posts` before including. `hidden: true` in a post's front matter keeps it out of the general listings — /writing and /archive — but NOT out of a tag page. It is not private: the post is still built, still at its own URL, still in the feed and the sitemap, and still findable in search, where it is marked as unlisted. It is for things worth having a link to but not worth putting in front of someone reading down the page. A tag page is not that. Nobody arrives at /tag/birthdays by scrolling; they asked for that subject by name, and answering with a short list — or an empty one — is answering a different question than the one put. So `show_hidden` turns the filter off, and _layouts/tagpage.html passes it. {%- endcomment -%}
