{%- comment -%} Shared post listing for the archive and tag pages. Caller sets `posts` before including. `hidden: true` in a post's front matter keeps it out of every listing on the site — this one, which is what /writing, /archive and every tag page render. It is not private: the post is still built, still at its own URL, still in the feed and the sitemap, and still findable in search, where it is marked as unlisted. It is for things worth having a link to but not worth putting in front of someone reading down the page. {%- endcomment -%}

The post stays here for its listing, its tags and its search entry; the link just lands on the project instead of on a post page. Same origin, so no target/rel — this opens in place like any other link. {%- endcomment -%}
